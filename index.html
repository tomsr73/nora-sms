Nora Alerts

Automated notifications by email and, optionally, text message from Nora, a self-hosted software application operated by Roderick Toms (sole proprietor).

Sign up

Enter your name and email address to receive Nora Alerts by email. Text messages are optional: to also get alerts by SMS, add your mobile number and check the separate SMS consent box. You can leave the phone number blank and the box unchecked and still receive every alert by email. Sign-up is limited to invited subscribers.

Who this is for

This is a private, non-commercial notification program for a small number of invited subscribers. Messages are generated automatically by the application's scheduler and monitoring rules, not written by a person. No phone numbers are bought, rented, or collected from third parties, and no marketing is sent.

What you will receive (by email, and by text if you opted in)

  • Automated alerts when a monitored check-in or scheduled task is missed or overdue.
  • Scheduled reminders.
  • Daily status and calendar summaries.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP (or CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE) at any time to stop all text messages; email alerts are unaffected unless you ask us to stop them too. You will receive one confirmation and nothing further. Reply START to re-subscribe. Reply HELP for help, or email noraai10141973@gmail.com.
